# Before the session

Three things, about ninety minutes of your time, spread over the week before. Nothing technical.

1. **Claude 101.** The short course your trainer links here: _(trainer adds the link)_. It explains what Claude is, what it is good at, and how to ask.
2. **Two short videos on the Claude desktop app, Code tab.** _(trainer adds two links)_. Watch how a folder is opened and how a sentence becomes an action.
3. **Install the app and try one thing.** Install Claude desktop, sign in with your work account, open any folder of your own files (a folder of Word documents is perfect), and type: *list the files here and tell me which one is newest*. Read the answer. That is the whole skill: open a folder, type a sentence, read the answer.

Bring to the session: your laptop with the app installed, and nothing else. Your trainer brings the project folder (or you open your own) and the credentials file.

Do: try step 3 twice, with two different sentences.
Don't: install anything else or set up any connection. The session covers that.

What to do next: the session itself. Afterwards, the take-home card and the rollout page. The deeper material under `reference/` is for later, if you want it.
