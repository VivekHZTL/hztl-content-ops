Three rules of the road

Read before yes. Every write shows environment, pages and changes first. If anything surprises you, say no.

Secrets stay in their file. Never paste a key, a password or a client secret into chat.

Production shouts. It needs its own credentials file, and every production write says PRODUCTION and asks again. Treat that prompt as a stop sign.

Thirty seconds. These three are also on the take-home card. Production is allowed for whoever holds the production file; the prompt makes sure nobody does it by accident.
